FAQ

Topview AI video creation FAQ.

Quick answers for creators, marketers, and teams exploring an agent-first production workflow.

What is Topview AI?

Topview is an AI Video Agent for short films, marketing videos, social content, image assets, and creative production workflows.

What is Canvas best for?

Canvas is best for planning scenes, organizing references, generating assets, and keeping every shot editable in context.

How does the Canvas agent turn an idea into a video?

It interprets the brief, proposes scenes, routes work to models, creates assets, and helps you refine the final direction.

Do beginners need video production skills?

No. Start with a sentence, a script, a URL, or a reference. The agent handles the first production pass while you guide the direction.

Can advanced creators still control the details?

Yes. You can choose references, models, prompts, scene direction, assets, and revisions as the project develops.

What is Drama Studio for?

Drama Studio is designed for micro-dramas, short series, and story-led production with consistent characters and scenes.

Can Topview recreate a reference video style?

Use a reference video to explore its pacing, hooks, shot structure, and creative language in a fresh direction.

How does Drama Studio keep episodes consistent?

Keep characters, scenes, props, prompts, continuity notes, dialogue, and visual references together as the story grows.

What is Board used for?

Board brings image, video, audio, and avatar outputs into one place for review, comparison, and reuse.

How is Topview different from generic AI video tools?

Topview connects planning, references, model routing, asset creation, revisions, and multi-format delivery in one workspace.
